CHARACTER DATA

  • Chihiro Rokuhira

    The protagonist character of Kagurabachi, he is the son of Kunishige Rokuhira and Chiaki Soga. After his father was murdered, he set out on a mission to retrieve the Enchanted Blades and defeating the Hishaku, the ones responsible for his death.

    • His birthday is August 11.
    • He's actually left handed, but can use his right effectively
    • Although his apperance and demeanour can seem cold, hes a very compassionate person.
    • First appeared in Chapter 1
    • Last seen in Chapter 112

  • Kunishige Rokuhira

    The father of Chihiro Rokuhira, he is regarded as the hero who ended the Seitei War, a great conflict between Japan and a forgotten clan, the Mikaboshi. He is the creator of the enchanted blades and was murdered by the Hishaku.

    • His birthday is June 5
    • He has terrible handwriting
    • His eyes are special, they regenerate and adapt
    • First appeared in Chapter 1
    • Last seen in Chapter 131

  • Togo Shiba

    Best friend of Kunishige and current guardian of Chihiro Rokuhira, Togo Shiba has now dedicated himself to helping Chihiro on his quest to recover the enchanted blades.

    • His birthday is October 15
    • Despite all hes been through he remains a laid back guy
    • Even as a kid he was very talented
    • Acted as a wingman for Chiaki and Kunishige
    • First appeared in Chapter 1
    • Last seen in Chapter 131

  • Hakuri Sazanami

    Hakuri is the son of Kiyora Sazanami and current wielder of their special ability, while he was growing up he was seen as nothing but a failure and abused by his older brother and discouraged by his family. He found his worth and friendship aftet meeting Chihiro and he continues to help him.

    • His birthday is March 27
    • He is the once in a generation genius who possesses both unique family abilites
    • Although he seems timid, he's a very determined person
    • First appeared in Chapter 19
    • Last seen in Chapter 121

  • Hinao

    Hinao is the owner of Cafe Haru Haru, which is used to give sorcerers business from employers. She heavily assists Chihiro and Shiba with information when needed.

    • Her birthday is December 21
    • She is the first major female character of the series
    • First appeared in Chapter 3
    • Last appeared in Chapter 45

  • Char Kyonagi

    An orphan and last remaining member of the Kyonagi clan, she like the others of her clan possess the ability of powerful regeneration. Due to that, shes been abducted and almost experimented on but thanks to Chihiro and the bonds she has formed, she is protected and seemingly happy.

    • Her birthday is May 12
    • Her appetite is massive
    • Many fans were shocked to learn she was blond
    • First Appeared in Chapter 3
    • Last seen in Chapter 45

  • Hiyuki Kagari

    The kamunabis strongest warrior and wielder of the Flame Bone of the Starving, a power that matches the enchanted blades. While she is a nice and moral person who wants what's best for the people, when she has strong opponent her battle thirst shows.

    • Some fans confused her gender during her debut
    • She was the first in 400 years to wield Flame Bone
    • First seen in Chapter 18
    • Chapter 123(as a baby)

  • Yoji Uruha

    The first sword bearer we meet, his is contract was with Enchanted Blade Kumeyuri. He was selected at 18 and said to be a prodigy in his youth. He holds a great respect for Kunishige Rokuhira and which he now shows through helping Chihiro.

    • His birthday is Febuary 12
    • One of the few wielders of Iai white purity style
    • He was first seen in Chapter 18 but fully introduced in Chapter 47
    • Last seen in Chapter 109

  • Seichi Samura

    Wielder of the Enchanted Blade Tobimune, Seichi Samura was a master swordsman and father of Iori Samura. He was one of the legendary heroes during the Seitei War although he holds deep regret for everything that happen at the end of it. He wanted a peaceful life for his daughter and atonement for his actions and that of the sword saint.

    • His birthday is September 7
    • Blinded himself due to trauma
    • First seen in Chapter 18 but fully introduced in Chapter 50
    • Last appeared in Chapter 111

  • Akemura Soga

    The legendary sword saint and wielder of the Magatsumi. Started a prodigy of the blade and one of the turning points within the war, though after the war he would commit genocide on all the people of Shokoku for currently unknown reasons. He is the brother of Chiakai Soga and brother-in-law of Kunishige Rokuhira.

    • He's always been resolute in his convictions and ideals.
    • While shown to be very serious and determined it seems he does have a goofier side to him.
    • First appeared in Chapter 18 but fully introduced in Chapter 100
    • Last seen in Chapter 130

  • Chiaki Soga

    As princess of the Soga clan, she wields the ability to gain prophecies. She is the sister of Akemura Soga, wife of Kunishige Rokuhira, mother of Chihiro Rokuhira, best friends with Soshiro Azami and Togo Shiba. Due to her ability, she was closed off from talking with her friends and became isolated and only a tool for the Soga Clan.

    • She is from a branch family and not the main, meaning most of the clan despises that the main branch didn't receive the power she has.
    • Due to how she was treated, she developed a complete lack of care for her own life in exchange for protecting others as seen when she states she is fine with being given away.
    • First appeared in Chapter 114
    • Last seen in Chapter 131

  • Yura

    A mysterious man who is the leader of the Hishaku, not much is known about his past or any of the Hishaku. He conducted the plan of killing Kunishige Rokuhira and taking the Enchated Blades. Although not a fighter he is very quick witted and nimble making him a difficult opponent. His goal as he states is "To bring order to this nation".

    • One translation of his name can mean Ghost, quite fitting for phantoms like the Hishaku who have no history.
    • First appeared in Chapter 7 and fully introduced in Chapter 48
    • Last seen in Chapter 112

  • Iori Samura

    Daughter of Seichi Samura, as a child she had her memories hidden by Samura and the Masumi for her own protection and went on to live a normal life, until the Hishaku targeted her as a bargaining chip for her father. She regained her memories and although she doesn't get to spend much time with her father after due to his demise at the hands of Akemura Soga. The last thing she recieved was the Tobimune blade and she is now an ally to Chihiro.

    • Her birthday is April 2
    • She is a wonderful sword user, she mimicks her fathers style very well
    • First appeared in Chapter 61 but fully named in Chapter 62
    • Last seen Chapter 112

  • Natsuki Misaka

    An incredible swordsman who is the brother of former wielder of Cloud Gouger,Ibuki Misaka. He is a commander within the Kamunabi and RIVALS(one-sided) with Yoji Uruha, who was selected for an Enchated Blade over him. He played a crucial role during the Hishaku invasion on Kamunabi HQ and remains an ally for the future.

    • Despite looking like an early adult or late teen, hes 36.
    • Holds a grudge with Uruha as he was selected over him but seems to respect him deeply.
    • First appearance in Chapter 89
    • Last seen in Chapter 110

  • Soshiro Azami

    One of the current leaders of the Kamunabi and childhood friends with Togo Shiba and Kunishige Rokuhira, Azami currently helps Chihiro to the recover the blades and continues to help him with any information he gathers while at the Kamunabi.

    • His birthday is April 24
    • Has the name of the Executioner and is regarded as one of the strongest members
    • First appeared in Chapter 7
    • Last seen in Chapter 110

  • Ikuto Hagiwara

    The leader of the Anti-Cloud Gouger squad who assisted Chihiro in his fight against Sojo, although most of his team dies he lives and his mental state declines due to it. Even in his damage state he continues to help the Kamunabi when they are raided by the hishaku showing he is a truly formidable leader.

    • He is subconsiously using his sorcery to project an image of his dead childhood friend.
    • His return was quite the shocker for many fans upon reading
    • First appeared in Chapter 10 and fully introduced in Chapter 12
    • Last seen in Chapter 101

  • Kiri Shirikai

    A skilled Kamunabi swordswoman who happens to be the granddaughter of the legendary master, Itsuo Shirikai. She helps protect Hakuri during the invasion of the Hishaku where we learn her goal is to kill her grandfather.

    • Chose an Odachi to spite her sexist grandfather and uses it rather effectively
    • Known for being casual and somewhat rude with others regardless of their superiority
    • First seen in Chapter 80 and fully introduced in Chapter 88
    • Last seen in Chapter 99

  • Tafuku Mihara

    A kamunabi agent who accompanies Hiyuki Kagari on most of her missions. While she is hot headed and full of energy, he seems to be laid back but does his job.

    • Although their personalities are polar opposites, Tafuku and Hiyuki work very effectively together
    • First appeared in Chapter 20 fully introduced in Chapter 21
    • Last seen in Chapter 46

  • The Masumi

    The Masumi are a lineage of ninja who help the kamunabi. We currently have seen 6 members, with the 3 current members being assigned to protect Seichi Samura. Following his death they have now allied with Chihiro and Iori. The 3 members we have names for are Ro, who happens to be the current clan head, Moku, and Sumi.

    • We have pre seitei war and current members
    • First apperance in Chapter 50
    • Last seen in Chapter 131

  • Kuguri

    A kamunabi member who was tasked with kidnapping Iori. Hes a bloodthirsty guy who loves clashing blades and holds swordsmanship to a high regard. Though he puts on a stoic face, he will forget the mission if it means a good battle.

    • Even with his crazy bloodlust, if he knows hes too outskilled he will value his life as seen when Samura was near.
    • First appeared in Chapter 61
    • Last seen in Chapter 78

  • Hiruhiko

    A member of the hishaku who is very playful and doesn't seem to take much serious unless its combat. While hes very combat intelligent, he doesn't have much experience with people and is quite delusional of how things work as seen with his interaction with Chihiro.

    • Commonly referred to as Hiruhik0-3 as he can only kill grunts.
    • First appeared in Chapter 48
    • Last seen in Chapter 78

  • Hokuto

    A member of the Hishaku. He is a very skilled swordsman and fighter even managing to defeat former sword bearer Ibuki Misaka and claims to be the one who landed the finishing blow on Kunishige Rokuhira. While watching his fights its clear he is very good with the sword and hes very obsessed with fighting strong opponents regardless of if he is outmatched as seen when he learns Uruha is alive and his battle with him.

    • Among the top 3 best looking characters in the series(fact not opinion)
    • First appeared in Chapter 2(puppet sorcery) but fully introduced in Chapter 89
    • Last seen in Chapter 109

  • Uran

    A Hishaku member who was among the 3 who murdered Kunishige Rokuhira. Like much of the other Hishaku not much is known of her past. From what was seen in her, her sorcery is ice manipulation as she was seen freezing a Kamunabi member completely. She also is very playful and seems to get along well with her fellow members.

    • One of the strongest Hishaku members
    • First seen in Chapter 2 fully introduced in Chapter 93
    • Last seen in Chapter 98

  • Bingo

    Bingo is a member of the Hishaku. He is one of the main forces during the raid on Kamunabi HQ. He seems to be a calm person and his sorcery is quite unique as he can transfom his head into a lion dancer head. Like all Hishaku, none of his past is known.

    • He has to eat people for his ability to work.
    • First appearance in Chapter 87, fully introduced in Chapter 93
    • Last seen in Chapter 98

  • Toto

    A member of the Hishaku who was paired with Kuguri to kidnap Iori Samura. She is more thoughtful and when a battle or mission is lost and more risks aren't needed, she doesn't mind retreating. As with all Hishaku, none of her past or current life is known.

    • Seems caring of her fellow members
    • First seen in Chapter 61, fully introduced in Chapter 65
    • Last seen in Chapter 78

  • Kyora Sazanami

    Former head of the Sazanami clan and overseer of the Rakuzaichi auction. He is also father of Hakuri Sazanami and his siblings. Kyora was stoic and cunning, putting his all into ensuring his duties were fulfilled. Due to that he wasn't much of a father figure in the natural way, as seen when he views Hakuri as a failure and useless. He wieled the Magatsumi for a short time before shortly being consumed by it and succumbing to his injuries.

    • First person we see wield the Magatsumi(in the series not in chronological timeline)
    • Fully introduced in Chapter 21
    • Last seen in Chapter 131

  • Soya Sazanami

    Brother to Hakuri Sazanami and Son to Kyora Sazanami, this guy is a complete psycho path. He abused and tortured Hakuri and claims its love. Even tho he's a complete psycho with Hakuri, around most others hes a somewhat normal guy with a very high intelligence. He also possessess Isou and has some insane durability. While we first thought he had perished, his story continues on(unfortunately).

    • First villain who was presumably killed to be shown alive
    • First appeared in Chapter 23
    • Last seen in Chapter 112.5

  • Tenri Sazanami

    A child of the Sazanami clan, Hakuris younger brother and a membeer of Tou. He was very dedicated like his father to fulfilling his duty of the Rakuzachi. He was also very obsessed with making his father proud. He fights against Chihiro and his crew during the Rakuzaichi Arc and battles Shiba.

    • 16 Years Old
    • Birthday is Febuary 8
    • First seen in Chapter 23 but fully introduced in Chapter 33
    • Last seen in Chapter 43

  • Enji Sazanami

    A member of the Sazanami clan and Tou.

    • Somehow placed highly on the fan poll LMAO
    • First appeared in Chapter 23
    • Last appeared in Chapter 44

  • Tamaki Sazanami

    One of the Sazanami clans Tou members, she is an antagonist during the Rakuzaichi arc.

    • Like with Enji, they don't seem to be Kyoras biological kids. More of relatives.
    • First appeared in Chapter 23
    • Last seen in Chapter 37

  • Ukizane Soga

    Legendary Soga swordsman who defeated the Mikaboshi one thousand years ago. With his sorcery, Kurotsuchi, destroying their island and forcing them to retreat to an underwater habitat.

    • Arguably the strongest characters shown
    • First appeared in Chapter 117
    • Last seen in Chapter 119

  • Yuuki

    A child who annoys Genichi Sojo during his bathhouse adventure.

    • First appeared in Chapter 92.1
    • Last seen in Chapter 92.1

  • Genichi Sojo

    The first main antagonist of Kagurabachi, a well known weapons dealer and once wielded the Cloud Gouger enchanted blade.

    • His birthday is June 6
    • First appeared in Chapter 5
    • Last seen in Chapter 112

  • Mr Inazuma

    A young boy who wishes to save his sister from the Rakuzaichi auction. A very brave hero.

    • VERY Popular Character
    • Potential future enchanted blade wielder???
    • First appeared in Chapter 27
    • Last seen in Chapter 45
