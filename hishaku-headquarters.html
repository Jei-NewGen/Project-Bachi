GET TO KNOW TAKERU HOKAZONO, AUTHOR OF KAGURABACHI

  • -Hokozono is very young and began to take drawing seriously only in college!
  • -His one shot manga "Enten" won him an award in which MY HERO ACADEMIA author Kohei Horikoshi was a judge!
  • -Hokazono is a very big fan of western movies, you can find aspects in the fights and narratives within Kagurabachi!
  • -He's a massive Naruto fan!
  • -He likes to think on his feet, so he takes long walks to form chapter layouts!
  • -Hokazono draws it all digitally!
